# `ocean-sunset`

![`ocean-sunset`](../media/ocean-sunset.webp)

Golden hour at sea. The sun rests on the horizon beyond a dark pine headland, heaped cloud overhead lit from below, a glitter path running across the water toward us, and long swells rolling in, their crests catching the light.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. It holds the cover view; `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/ocean_sunset.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/ocean_sunset.rs).

![`ocean-sunset-wide`](../media/ocean-sunset-wide.webp)
