# `aurora-fjord`

![`aurora-fjord`](../media/aurora-fjord.gif)

Curtains of aurora ripple over a fjord between snowy mountains. The still water holds a broken shimmer of them, and a red cabin on the far shore keeps its lamps lit.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. It holds the cover view; `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/aurora_fjord.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/aurora_fjord.rs).
