# `taj-dawn`

![`taj-dawn`](../media/taj-dawn.gif)

The Taj Mahal at first light, seen down its long reflecting canal between rows of cypress. The sun has just cleared the red sandstone mosque on the left; the haze and the clouds drift, the canal's reflection ripples, light glints along its far end, and a few birds cross.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. It holds the cover view; `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/taj_dawn.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/taj_dawn.rs).
