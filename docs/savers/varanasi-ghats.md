# `varanasi-ghats`

![`varanasi-ghats`](../media/varanasi-ghats.gif)

Dusk on the Ganga. Stepped ghats run along the bank and away toward the afterglow, temple spires black against an indigo to amber sky. Priests raise the aarti lamps on the steps, diyas drift downstream on the dark water, and a boatman rows slowly across the bright reach.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. It holds the cover view; `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/varanasi_ghats.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/varanasi_ghats.rs).
