# `deep-reef`

![`deep-reef`](../media/deep-reef.webp)

Looking along a coral reef from a few metres down. The sun is a bright blaze in the rippled surface, shafts of light fan down from it, kelp sways in the swell, a school of fish wheels through the dark water, bubbles rise and caustics crawl over the sand.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. It holds the cover view; `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/deep_reef.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/deep_reef.rs).
