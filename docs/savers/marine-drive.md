# `marine-drive`

The Queen's Necklace seen from Malabar Hill at night. A string of sodium lamps sweeps round the bay to the towers at Nariman Point, cars trail light along the road beneath them, and the bay carries their long wavering reflections. Ships ride at anchor on the horizon.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness. The camera slowly tours it; the shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md).

Source: [`src/ascii_rest/marine_drive.rs`](../../src/ascii_rest/marine_drive.rs).

## `marine-drive-wide`

A longer necklace and skyline, a liner and a fishing boat past the point.

Recomposed at 320x100 (3.2:1) to fill pine's glass uncropped; see [`-wide` variants](../ascii-rest.md#-wide-variants).
