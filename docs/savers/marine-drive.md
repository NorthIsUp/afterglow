# `marine-drive`

![`marine-drive`](../media/marine-drive.gif)

The Queen's Necklace seen from Malabar Hill at night. A string of sodium lamps sweeps round the bay to the towers at Nariman Point, cars trail light along the road beneath them, and the bay carries their long wavering reflections. Ships ride at anchor on the horizon.

One of ascii.rest's thirteen halftone scenes: shaded cell by cell and drawn as dots whose size is their brightness, composed for whatever panel it lands on. `ASCII_REST_TOUR=1` sets a camera slowly touring it. The shared engine, the tour and its knobs are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md#scenes).

## At any size

The bay always runs from the hill at the left round to the point, with the freighter anchored just past it and the moon over the open sea. On a panel wider than upstream's 2:1 the bay lengthens, so the necklace carries more lamps and blocks, more towers rise mid-curve, and the open sea past the point takes a fishing boat from about 2.3:1 and a liner under the moon from about 2.7:1. On a narrower one (4:3, square) the bay shortens to fewer blocks and a tighter cluster at the point. On a portrait panel the extra rows are sky above, where the moon climbs, and bay below.

## Knobs

None of its own; `ASCII_REST_TOUR*` and `ASCII_REST_TITLE` are in [the ascii.rest ports](../ascii-rest.md#scenes). On upstream's 200x100 grid it is upstream's picture cell for cell.
